# Tutor App — Product Context

## Product Purpose
A live AI voice tutoring application for middle and high school students. The student speaks with an AI tutor (Gemini Live) in real time while a shared whiteboard (Tldraw) renders equations, diagrams, and visual reasoning. Feels like a knowledgeable friend at a physical whiteboard.

## Register
product

## Users
Middle and high school students (12–18) studying math, physics, chemistry, writing, history. Often stressed about homework or exams. Using on a laptop at a desk or kitchen table. Sessions are 15–45 minutes.

## Tone
Warm, focused, calm. Intelligent without being cold. Not childish. Not corporate.

## Key Surface: Session Page
A full-screen split layout during an active tutoring session:
- **Left/main:** Tldraw whiteboard canvas (infinite, scrollable)
- **Right/overlay:** Session controls panel

### Session Panel Features
- Live transcript (AI tutor messages as bullet points, student speech in italic)
- File upload / materials dropzone (homework, notes, slides)
- Mute microphone button (keyboard shortcut M)
- End session button
- Live status indicator + elapsed session timer

## Anti-References
- Bright primary-color "edtech" apps (Kahoot!, Duolingo) — too gamified
- Generic LMS interfaces (Canvas, Schoology) — too corporate/flat
- Cold productivity dashboards (Linear, Vercel) — too sterile
